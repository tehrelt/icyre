Transport controls: play/pause, previous, next, shuffle, repeat.

**Provide:** `kind`, `playing` (play), `active` (shuffle), `repeatMode` (`off` | `all` | `one`), `size` (`md` 48 / `lg` 64 for play), `loading`, `disabled`, `onClick`.

- Play/pause is the round iridescent button — the most frequent brand touchpoint. The glyph morphs with `easing-emphasized`.
- Shuffle/repeat on: `text-brand` color **plus** a 4px dot beneath (and a "1" chip for repeat-one), with `aria-pressed`.
